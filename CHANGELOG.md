# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- The Cultist Circle button now sits with the other hideout buttons when Vertical Hideout is installed, instead of on top of the area list. Thanks to Recker for reporting it and testing the fix.

## 1.0.0

First release. Split out of zezinho's Shopping List.

- Optimizer: pick the ritual time and a safety level, get the cheapest items from traders and the flea market that reach it, with the chance of each time.
- Three options per plan, skip and ban per item, optional use of junk from your stash, safe flea offers.
- Buys with live status and a retry, then moves the items into the circle.
- Recipes with inputs, rewards, times, cost, value and profit.
- Results: the roll, tier and rewards of each ritual, and the profit or loss.
- Excluded items, editable in the window and in `circle-exclusions.json`.
- Follows other mods' circle settings (Cultist Circle Improvements, SVM) because it reads them live.
- Only appears once the hideout's Cultist Circle is built. English and Portuguese.
